**Personas** — simulation voices for stressor-walk and FMEA, distinguished as `walk-persona` (project-state concerns from a stakeholder role) or `defense-persona` (a simulated outside audience rehearsing how residuality artifacts will land). Record at least two distinct personas before deep analysis — verification nudges when fewer exist but does not block capture.

Two nominally-different personas that share the same blind spots are not coverage — they are one persona wearing two labels. Prefer personas whose incentives genuinely diverge (an on-call engineer vs. a product owner vs. a security auditor) over personas that differ only in job title. When every persona in a walk agrees quickly, treat that as a signal to add a persona that would plausibly disagree — not as a signal the walk is done. This is the practical guard against A-18 (Perspective Coverage Fidelity): nominal persona count is not the same as actual viewpoint diversity.
