**Purposes** — behavioral contracts that must hold for the positive attractor state to remain stable; the complement to stressors (what you intend vs. what the world may do). Each purpose names a feature or behavior whose absence or breakage moves the system toward the negative attractor state.

**Purposes are not features.** A feature is a unit of shipped functionality — a checkbox on a roadmap. A purpose is the reason that feature matters, framed as an attractor-preserving contract: what would go wrong, and toward which negative state, if it silently stopped holding. Two features can share one purpose; one feature can serve zero purposes (pure plumbing, dead weight, or scaffolding). Do not promote every feature ticket to a purpose by default — a purpose earns its place by naming the attractor it protects.
