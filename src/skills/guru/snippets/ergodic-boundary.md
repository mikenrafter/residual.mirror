**Ergodic boundary** — the boundary, redrawn per architecture iteration, between the ergodic core under architectural consideration and the non-ergodic torrent of the hyperliminal outside it. Behavior explained *within* the boundary by known components interacting is a **component dynamic** — a bug, a local refactor, a performance tune — and belongs in code review, not the force ledger. Pressure originating *beyond* the boundary — a market shift, an organizational change, a dependency's maintainers vanishing, a whole class of user behavior nobody modeled — is architecture-level: a stressor or purpose.

The boundary is not fixed. As an architecture iteration matures, what was once "beyond the boundary and unknowable" can become a known component dynamic worth handling locally, and the boundary should move inward to match. Before recording a stressor, ask which side of the *current* iteration's boundary it sits on — if it is fully explained by known components, it is a component dynamic (and possibly a bug), not a stressor yet.
