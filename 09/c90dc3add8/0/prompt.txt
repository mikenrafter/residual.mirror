## Key design decisions (documented in file-header comments)

- `SnapshotAttractor` gained a `description` field beyond the task's sketch — `add attractor --description` is `required: true` in `web/generated/cli-schema.json`, so it's needed to ever emit a valid line.
- Required-fields ground truth is `cli-schema.json`, not the plan prose: `add stressor`/`add purpose` require `description`, `attractor-id`, `naive-change`/`feature` but **not** `outcomes` (schema says `required: false`) — `isForceValid` deliberately excludes outcomes.
- `add stressor`/`add purpose` have no `--add-component` flag in the schema — only `update` does — so a brand-new force's component toggles always surface as a synthetic follow-up `update &lt;type&gt; --force-id &lt;tempId&gt; --add-component ...` entry in the update phase, keyed by the force's `tempId`.
- Terms are tracked as their own bucket, ordered last in both add and update phases (plan's ordering rule only names components/attractors/personas/forces).
